# Go-to-market — 90-day operating hypothesis

## Positioning
Broad audience **by design**, narrow entry by *use-case*. Do not pitch 'unlimited AI agents' as primary benefit. Sell: 'Create an AI team for repetitive writing, research, documents and business communications, under your control'. Launch Israel with Hebrew, support English later, use self-service plus concierge to learn early.

## Acquisition lanes (parallel, not separate products)
1. Direct outreach: B2B owner/operators using marketing, customer response or document-heavy work. Sell one measurable use-case.
2. Content/SEO: short demonstrations 'from brief to checked output', comparisons and how-to guides tied to template library.
3. Organic demos: webinar, LinkedIn/Facebook owner communities, launch videos with real results (anonymized, consented).
4. Referral / integrations partner (later): consultants bringing teams, but billed directly to end business, not reseller white-label.

## Timeline (goals, not guarantees)
**Days 1–14: validate pain and message**
- 15 interviews across at least 5 business sectors; 10 pain statements and willingness-to-pay probes.
- Publish landing/waitlist with explicit non-GA status; track interest, avoid promising unsupported features.
- 2 clickable demos using synthetic data; identify 3 universally useful templates.
- Define payment/legal requirements with accountant/legal adviser and shortlist provider.
Exit gate: >=5 participants agree to a timed pilot, >=3 prepared to pay if measurable result; if absent, adjust positioning.

**Days 15–30: first product slice and beta recruiting**
- Build auth/workspaces, agent studio, one working provider and 3 templates (at least one sequential team).
- 5 design partners from different sectors, recorded baseline time/task and desired output quality.
- Onboarding copy and funnel analytics. No claim that billing is live until sandbox + approvals pass.
Exit gate: at least 3 pilot users successfully complete task without developer intervention; acceptable quality and latency under stated constraints.

**Days 31–60: commercial safeguards and controlled paid beta**
- Implement plan entitlements, metering, verified sandbox payment flows, super-admin, consent/terms/privacy, user data deletion and security testing.
- Test with 3 paid design partners after live payment validation; charge only for delivered scope and provide clear support.
- Track weekly activation, 7-day repeat use, value delivered, error cost, direct gross margin.
Exit gate: accurate billing, cross-org isolation, no unauthorized external actions, demo-to-run success and no critical incidents.

**Days 61–90: public launch candidate**
- Productize web sign-up, templates, onboarding, billing portal, support, monthly subscriptions.
- Publish 8 helpful demos, 4 case studies only with consent, A/B test headline and 'first-value' funnel.
- Target 10 paying retained organizations and repeat usage by end period **if launch gates pass**; not a forecast or promise.
- Decide whether to scale ads based on CAC payback and contribution margin; no blanket ad spend commitment.

## KPI targets to test (not benchmarks)
- First useful result under 10min median among successful activations.
- First-run success >90% on supported template tests with actual providers.
- 7-day repeat-use >35% for activated beta accounts.
- Trial→paid >10% after meaningful use and billing validation.
- Paid 30-day gross churn <7% (small sample highly noisy); target direct gross margin >=70% at chosen plan mix.
- CAC payback target <6 months when CAC is measurable with enough data.
If metrics fail, fix retention and cost model before advertising broadly.

## Early sales script
'איזו עבודה חוזרת אצלכם צורכת לפחות שעתיים בשבוע? אני רוצה להראות איך אפשר להפוך אותה לתהליך AI עם אישור ובקרה, בלי להעביר החלטות רגישות אוטומטית.'
Offer 20-minute demo of one real use case; measure before/after by user-confirmed hours/quality, not unverified ROI claims.

## Spend guard
For initial experiment propose budget split as variable placeholders to be approved; don't commit spend without business cash constraints. Prioritize direct validation and organic outreach before paid acquisition. Cost dashboard includes monthly provider/API spend, infrastructure, payment processing, support time and refunds.
